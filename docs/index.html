Call Blocker

Incoming calls from numbers that are not in your contacts are rejected. Nothing else.

Download the APK Source on GitHub

What it does

  • Rejects incoming calls from numbers not saved in your contacts.
  • Withheld and private numbers are blocked too — there is no number to match.
  • Optionally silences instead of rejecting, so callers reach voicemail normally.
  • Keeps a local list of recently blocked calls so you can see what you missed.

Why it is lightweight

The app has zero third-party dependencies and uses Android's own CallScreeningService — no analytics, no ad SDKs, nothing running in the background between calls.

Requires Android 10 or newer. Older versions only let the default phone app screen calls. Android also permits one call-screening app at a time, so enabling this replaces your current one.

Privacy

It collects nothing and has no internet permission at all. See the privacy policy.
